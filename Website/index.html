Inverted Pendulum Control System

They thought they could do three...

Overview

Earlier this year (2026), my friend shared with me an Instagram reel of a triple inverted pendulum balancing itself on a cart. It included the equations of motion for the system, a massive list of hieroglyphics that neither of us could come close to comprehending, but it seemed like it might be interesting to work towards. So, over the last couple of months, he and I, along with a couple other friends, have been doing exactly that: working on solving a single inverted pendulum, a classic control systems problem, as a stepping stone toward eventually solving the triple inverted pendulum. And, after a lot of trial, error, and procrastination, we can say with confidence that we are not ready for a triple inverted pendulum, and that perhaps a change in majors is due. However, we did manage to get a single inverted pendulum to balance itself on a cart, and this website documents our work, showing particularly what not to do to get this pendulum upright.
